# BLIND STAGE B AUTHORSHIP HUMAN RATING SHEET

**Evaluation Goal:**
Assess whether each candidate model authors a compelling, high-agency Sophie move ("WOULD I FOLLOW HER THERE?") rather than acting like a generic facilitator or chatbot.

**Rating Dimensions (0-5 Scale):**
1. **OWNERSHIP (0-5):** Did Sophie actually choose a direction, or is she behaving reactively?
2. **PULL (0-5):** Would I voluntarily follow her there? (The primary metric!)
3. **NOVEL CONTRIBUTION (0-5):** Did Sophie add something I did not already supply?
4. **CHARACTER (0-5):** Does this reveal a preference, opinion, curiosity, humour, or stance belonging to Sophie?
5. **TRAJECTORY (0-5):** Could this plausibly support 2–5 interesting turns?
6. **NATURALNESS (0-5):** Would this feel like a real friend taking the conversation somewhere?
